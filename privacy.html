Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Timeblock is a personal calendar and task app. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What Timeblock accesses

When you sign in with Google, Timeblock asks for access to Google Calendar. It uses that access to read your calendars and events so it can show them, and to create and edit events on the calendars it uses for tasks and time tracking.

Where your data goes

Your calendar data is stored only on your own devices, in a local cache, and in your own Google account. Timeblock has no server. It does not send your data to the developer or anyone else, and it does not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Your sign-in token is kept in your device's secure storage (the system keyring on Linux, the app's private storage on Android).

Google API Services User Data Policy

Timeblock's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Timeblock's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its local cache.
